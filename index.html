Who we are

From trading chemicals to making zinc derivatives.

Ambica Chemicals began in 2010 trading organic and inorganic chemicals. With the aim to diversify and grow, we moved into manufacturing as an MSME, building our name on zinc derivatives.

Our plant in Hooghly district runs modern facilities with an in-house testing lab, so every consignment — from Ammonium Chloride to Zinc Dust Powder — leaves with the quality we promised.
