Our story

Started in 2010. Still obsessed with purity.

Our story began in 2010 with the trading of organic and inorganic chemicals. To diversify and grow, Ambica Chemicals moved on to establish itself as an MSME producing zinc derivatives.

Our manufacturing plant sits in Hooghly district, West Bengal, with modern facilities and an in-house testing lab. We aim to be a leading, trusted name for a comprehensive range of chemical compounds — pure, effective and quality assured, and easy for customers in pharmaceutical, agricultural, textile and many other sectors to procure.
